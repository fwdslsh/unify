Where

The forge sits at the back of the old tannery yard, down the lane behind the millpond. Look for the anvil sign on the gate — the door is the one with the chimney over it.

Safety rules

  • Closed-toe shoes and natural-fibre clothing at all times near the forge.
  • Long hair tied back; no loose sleeves, scarves, or dangling jewellery.
  • Safety glasses on at the anvil, full face shield for grinding.
  • One person on the fire at a time; wait for the instructor's nod before you strike.
  • Under-16s must be accompanied by a supervising adult throughout.
