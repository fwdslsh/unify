Good turnout for Tool Care this month, and for the first time we let people
bring in their own chisels and drawknives instead of practising only on the
shop set. Worked well — people care more about an edge when it's theirs.

A few notes:

- The coarse stone by the sharpening bench is nearly worn to the wood.
  Ordered a replacement; until it arrives, start on the medium stone instead.
- Remind people that a burr you can feel with a fingernail is a burr you
  haven't finished removing.
- One visitor asked about lawnmower blades. Politely no — wrong steel, wrong
  class.

Thistleknap keeps its loaner tool set locked in the blue cabinet; ask an
instructor rather than digging through the open shelf, which is for finished
member projects only.
