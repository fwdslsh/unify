Six of eight in Saturday's Forge Basics group had never lit a coal fire before.
By the second hour everyone could bank a fire and draw a decent taper without
being told twice.

Two things worth writing down for next time:

- The new bag of coal burns hotter than the last one. Tell people to expect a
  smaller fire than they think they need.
- Nobody remembered to quench between heats until reminded. Say it earlier in
  the safety briefing next time, not just once at the start.

Thistleknap keeps a spare set of tongs by the far anvil now, so the taper
station doesn't have to share with the hook station anymore.
