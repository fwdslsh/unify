First outing for the Heat Treatment course since we rewrote it to require
Forge Basics as a prerequisite, and it showed — nobody needed the fire
behaviour explained from scratch, so we got through normalising, hardening,
and tempering colours in one session instead of two.

Notes for the next group:

- The magnet test for non-magnetic (critical) temperature is still the
  easiest tell for this group's eyes. Keep leading with it before talking
  about colour.
- Tempering in the kitchen oven downstairs works, but it's slow to recover
  temperature with the door open. Suggest smaller batches per bake.
- Two people asked about oil quenching smell/smoke — worth a line in the
  safety briefing before it happens, not after.

Thistleknap keeps oil-quench tanks away from the coal fire, on the wall
opposite the door, for exactly this reason.
