Thistleknap Forge

Thistleknap Forge is a community blacksmithing collective. We keep a working coal forge open to newcomers and old hands alike, teach the basics to anyone willing to get hot and sooty, and lend bench space to members finishing their own projects.

The forge runs on volunteer hours and modest course fees. Nobody here is paid; everybody here is glad you came.

Thistleknap keeps its fire lit every session the instructors can staff it, rain or shine.
