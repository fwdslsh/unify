How lending works

Borrowing seed from Fernhollow works like borrowing a book, with one difference: we hope you don't bring back the same seed you took.

Borrowing

  1. Browse the catalogue and pick a variety your household has room to grow.
  2. Take one packet per variety, per member, per season — that keeps stock spread across the membership instead of one grower taking it all.
  3. Sign the packet out with a seed keeper at the potting shed, or ask a keeper to post it if you can't get in.

Growing and returning

Grow the variety through to seed if you're able, following the notes on its catalogue page for sowing time and days to maturity. At the end of the season, save what seed you can and return a share of it to the library — clean, dry, and labelled with the variety name and the year. If a crop fails, that's fine too; just let a seed keeper know so we can plan next season's stock.

Fernhollow keeps no fines and no waiting lists — if a variety is out of packets, a seed keeper will tell you when the next batch is expected.

Membership terms

Membership is free and open to anyone in the valley. By borrowing seed you agree to: grow it without chemical treatment, return a share of saved seed when you're able, and tell a seed keeper if a variety fails so the catalogue stays accurate for the next member. The library keeps members' contact details private and never shares them outside the library committee.
