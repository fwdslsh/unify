Bere Parsnip 18 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 20 packets of Bere Parsnip 18 (Cucurbita) in trust for members, ready to sow March–May and to harvest in about 125 days.
