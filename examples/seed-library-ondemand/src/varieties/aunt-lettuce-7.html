Aunt Lettuce 7 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 35 packets of Aunt Lettuce 7 (Poaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow March–August and to harvest in about 110 days.
