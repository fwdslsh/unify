Welsh Beet 8 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 24 packets of Welsh Beet 8 (Brassica) in trust for members, ready to sow June–May and to harvest in about 111 days.
