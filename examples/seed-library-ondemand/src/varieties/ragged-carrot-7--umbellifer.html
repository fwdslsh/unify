Ragged Carrot 7 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 25 packets of Ragged Carrot 7 (Umbellifer) in trust for members, ready to sow March–June and to harvest in about 140 days.
