Autumn Turnip 16 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 17 packets of Autumn Turnip 16 (Asteraceae) in trust for members, ready to sow February–June and to harvest in about 108 days.
