Champion Tomato 17 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 30 packets of Champion Tomato 17 (Asteraceae) in trust for members, ready to sow March–July and to harvest in about 115 days.
