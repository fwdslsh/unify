Nine Star Turnip 21 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 33 packets of Nine Star Turnip 21 (Brassica) in trust for members, ready to sow April–June and to harvest in about 112 days.
