Champion Leek 8 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 10 packets of Champion Leek 8 (Cucurbita) in trust for members, ready to sow March–July and to harvest in about 55 days.
