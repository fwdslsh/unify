Early Tomato 18 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 14 packets of Early Tomato 18 (Lamiaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow June–July and to harvest in about 91 days.
