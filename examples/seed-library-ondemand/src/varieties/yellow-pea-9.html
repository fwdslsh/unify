Yellow Pea 9 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 21 packets of Yellow Pea 9 (Poaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow May–June and to harvest in about 124 days.
