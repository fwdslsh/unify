Yellow Pea 17 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 21 packets of Yellow Pea 17 (Brassica) in trust for members, ready to sow May–June and to harvest in about 84 days.
