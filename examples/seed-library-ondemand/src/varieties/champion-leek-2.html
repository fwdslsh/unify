Champion Leek 2 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 10 packets of Champion Leek 2 (Poaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow March–July and to harvest in about 75 days.
