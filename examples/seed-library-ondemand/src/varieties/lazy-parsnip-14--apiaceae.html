Lazy Parsnip 14 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 36 packets of Lazy Parsnip 14 (Apiaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow May–May and to harvest in about 79 days.
