Champion Onion 12 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 10 packets of Champion Onion 12 (Umbellifer) in trust for members, ready to sow March–July and to harvest in about 85 days.
