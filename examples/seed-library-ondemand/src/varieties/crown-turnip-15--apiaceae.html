Crown Turnip 15 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 9 packets of Crown Turnip 15 (Apiaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow June–June and to harvest in about 86 days.
