Lazy Kale 10 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 16 packets of Lazy Kale 10 (Solanum) in trust for members, ready to sow May–May and to harvest in about 99 days.
