Pink Parsnip 16 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 28 packets of Pink Parsnip 16 (Lamiaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow April–May and to harvest in about 77 days.
