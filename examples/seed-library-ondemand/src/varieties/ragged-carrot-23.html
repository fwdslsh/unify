Ragged Carrot 23 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 25 packets of Ragged Carrot 23 (Solanum) in trust for members, ready to sow March–June and to harvest in about 100 days.
