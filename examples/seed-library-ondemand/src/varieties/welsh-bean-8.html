Welsh Bean 8 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 4 packets of Welsh Bean 8 (Lamiaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow June–May and to harvest in about 111 days.
