Red Russian Leek 20 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 26 packets of Red Russian Leek 20 (Solanum) in trust for members, ready to sow May–July and to harvest in about 79 days.
