Aunt Chard 14 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 15 packets of Aunt Chard 14 (Allium) in trust for members, ready to sow March–August and to harvest in about 140 days.
