Ragged Turnip 11 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 25 packets of Ragged Turnip 11 (Chenopod) in trust for members, ready to sow March–June and to harvest in about 130 days.
