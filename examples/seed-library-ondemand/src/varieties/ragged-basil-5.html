Ragged Basil 5 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 5 packets of Ragged Basil 5 (Lamiaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow March–June and to harvest in about 90 days.
