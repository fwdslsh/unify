Cherokee Chard 13 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 23 packets of Cherokee Chard 13 (Apiaceae) in trust for members, ready to sow April–August and to harvest in about 72 days.
