Welsh Kale 10 has been grown in the Fernhollow valley for generations and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Fernhollow keeps 24 packets of Welsh Kale 10 (Umbellifer) in trust for members, ready to sow June–May and to harvest in about 71 days.
