Fernhollow Seed Library

We lend vegetable seed to members across the valley, season after season. Borrow a packet, grow it out, and — when you can — save some seed to return so the next member can grow it too.

Fernhollow keeps 225 varieties across twelve plant families in the current catalogue, every one grown out by a member before it was ever offered for loan.
