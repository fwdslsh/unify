Redpoll

A river-level sensor you can hang off a footbridge in twenty minutes and leave there for a year.

Redpoll measures the distance to the water forty times a minute, keeps the median, and sends one packet an hour over LoRaWAN. It runs from a 2 W panel and a single cell, it reports its own battery and tilt, and it stores its calibration on the board so a replacement head can be swapped in on the bank without a laptop.

  • Range — 0.25 m to 8 m, ±3 mm at 2 m
  • Power — 2 W panel, 18650 cell, 14 months measured in shade
  • Radio — LoRaWAN 1.0.4, EU868 or US915
  • Housing — cast aluminium, IP67, 780 g on the bracket

Start with the quickstart, then calibrate against a staff gauge. Every firmware and hardware change is written up in the release notes.
