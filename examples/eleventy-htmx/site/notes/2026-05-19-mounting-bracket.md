# A bracket for parapets

The original bracket clamps a 48&nbsp;mm scaffold tube, which is perfect on a gauging
station and useless on a stone parapet. The new flat bracket takes two M8 bolts through
masonry or a handrail post and holds the head 180&nbsp;mm clear of the face.

The clearance is the whole design. On the two parapet sites we bodged before making this,
the sensor sat close enough to the stone that its own 30&nbsp;degree cone caught the face
below it, and both returned a rock-steady distance that had nothing to do with the river.
180&nbsp;mm clears a vertical face for the full working range.

It is the same 4&nbsp;mm hex fitting and the same tilt adjustment as the tube bracket, so
the [quickstart](/docs/quickstart.html) applies unchanged from step 3 onward. Aluminium,
anodised, 240&nbsp;g, and it ships with the bolts because nobody has ever had the right
bolts on the bank.

Both brackets stay available. If you have a tube, clamp it.
