# Firmware 2.5.0

**Tilt is now on every uplink**, as signed degrees in byte 5. The accelerometer was on the
board from the start and only being read at boot, which meant a bracket knocked in February
was invisible until someone visited in June. Watching tilt for a change of more than two
degrees has caught three struck brackets since we started running this internally.

**Join backoff is a flat fifteen minutes.** The old exponential backoff reached six hours
after a day of failing to join, so a sensor deployed before its keys were registered looked
dead for the rest of the week. Flat is worse in theory and much better in practice.

**`read` is a new console command** and prints distance, level, tilt and battery in one
line, which is what you actually want while standing on a bank holding a staff gauge. See
[calibration](/docs/calibration.html) for what to do with it.

One removal: the debug UART no longer prints raw sample arrays by default. It filled the
console at exactly the moment you needed to read something, and `samples verbose` turns it
back on.
