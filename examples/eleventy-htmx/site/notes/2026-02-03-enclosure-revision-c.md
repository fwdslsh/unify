# Enclosure revision C

Revision C is the last change we expect to make to the cast housing, and all three
differences come from things that went wrong on the bank rather than on the bench.

**The gland shoulder is 3&nbsp;mm deeper.** Two sites in the first winter took water past a
gland that had been tightened onto the cable's own moulded strain relief rather than onto
the jacket. The deeper shoulder makes the wrong assembly physically obvious instead of
merely wrong.

**The lid screws are captive.** Four M3 screws in a stiff breeze on a footbridge is four
chances to lose one; they now stay in the lid.

**The desiccant pocket opens from the side**, so a sachet can be changed without taking the
head off the bracket or breaking the transducer seal. Change it when you re-check
calibration and the seal never has to be broken in the field at all.

Revisions A and B take the same bracket and the same board. Nothing about this change
affects firmware, and a revision A housing is not due for replacement.
