title: "May update: Growing well"
---

**May 3, 2026**

Fernhollow keeps its rhythm with the seasons. May is when our collection transitions from seedling stage to established plants filling the garden.

## Transplanting complete

All the seedlings we raised in March and April are now in their growing positions:

- **Tomatoes:** Most varieties transplanted, establishing in warm spots
- **Beans:** Direct sown last week, germinating well
- **Squash:** Potted up, ready for hardening off and final planting
- **Brassicas:** Transplants looking sturdy with good root systems

## Cool-season crops finishing

First harvests happening:

- **Peas:** Champion of England and Carlin have been flowering; first picking this week
- **Broad beans:** Robinson Broad Bean plants are in flower
- **Spring onions:** Welsh Onion growing strongly for leaf harvest

## What keepers are reporting

**L. Barr** (Ailsa Craig onions): First onion sets visible, growing well in well-drained soil.

**M. Odhran** (Bedfordshire Champion): Also onion-growing, with good-sized sets developing. Planning to save seed in August from finest bulbs.

**J. Vance** (multiple varieties): Planning succession sowings of Yellow Pear tomatoes to extend harvest.

**R. Simms** (Chantenay Red carrots): First thinnings used for salads, excellent flavor in this variety.

## Pest watch

Mild pressure from slugs on young beans, but handpicking is keeping up with them. No disease issues.

## Planning for summer

We're receiving requests for summer-growing varieties. If you haven't yet collected your seeds, visit us this Saturday (10am–2pm) to select what you'd like to try growing.

## The season ahead

Everything is pointing toward a strong growing season. Regular watering will be key in the warmer weeks ahead.
