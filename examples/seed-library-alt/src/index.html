Welcome to Fernhollow Seed Library

Fernhollow keeps a living repository of heritage vegetable seeds, grown and shared by our community members. We believe in the importance of preserving biodiversity and supporting local food security through accessible seed sharing.

How it works

Our library is built on the principle of mutual aid. Members can borrow seeds, grow them, and return some of their harvest as seeds for the next growing season. Browse our catalogue of available varieties to find seeds that interest you.

What we offer

  • Over 25 heritage vegetable varieties
  • Access to seeds from dedicated keepers in our community
  • Growing guides for beginners and experienced gardeners
  • Seasonal updates on what's growing and ready

Getting started

Learn how our lending system works, then visit us on a Saturday morning to join and select your first seeds.
