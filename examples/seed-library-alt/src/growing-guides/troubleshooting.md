---
title: Troubleshooting Common Problems
---

Fernhollow keeps detailed records of how our varieties perform. If you're having trouble, you're not alone—these are common issues our members solve every season.

## Seedlings not sprouting

**Possible causes:**
- **Soil too cold:** Most seeds need 15°C minimum, many prefer 20°C
- **Soil too wet:** Wet compost causes seeds to rot before sprouting
- **Seeds too old:** Some seeds lose viability quickly; check the packet
- **Planted too deep:** Most seeds need light or only slight covering

**Solution:** Replace compost, water more carefully, keep warmer, and always read the packet.

## Seedlings looking pale and stretched

**Possible causes:**
- **Not enough light:** This is the most common issue indoors
- **Too much nitrogen:** Rich compost can cause weak growth
- **Overcrowded:** Seedlings compete for light and air

**Solution:** Move closer to light or provide artificial lighting. Thin seedlings to give them space. Avoid over-feeding.

## Plants bolting (going to seed too early)

**Possible causes:**
- **Temperature stress:** Sudden heat or cold can trigger flowering
- **Inconsistent watering:** Stressed plants bolt to reproduce
- **Wrong variety for season:** Some varieties are sensitive to day length

**Solution:** Keep watering consistent. Shade plants in extreme heat. Choose the right variety for your season (check with us!).

## Pest damage

### Aphids
Small insects that cluster on stems and leaves, causing sticky residue.
- Spray with water to dislodge
- Apply insecticidal soap
- Encourage ladybugs

### Caterpillars
Holes in leaves, droppings on plants.
- Hand-pick where possible
- Use butterfly-safe netting to prevent egg-laying
- Accept some damage—they become butterflies!

### Slugs and snails
Irregular holes, slime trails on leaves.
- Beer traps or copper barriers
- Remove plant debris they hide in
- Handpick at night

## Disease

### Powdery mildew
White dust on leaves, common in warm, dry conditions.
- Improve air circulation
- Water at soil level, not on leaves
- Remove affected leaves

### Damping off (seedlings collapse)
Wet compost and poor air circulation.
- Use fresh compost, not garden soil
- Water from below
- Improve ventilation

## Not getting seeds to mature

**Possible causes:**
- **Wrong variety for climate:** Some tomato varieties need more warmth
- **Timing too late:** If plants don't mature before frost
- **Poor pollination:** Beans and tomatoes need bees or hand-pollination

**Solution:** Choose varieties suited to our valley (ask us!). Sow early enough. Hand-pollinate indoors by shaking flowers.

## When to ask for help

We're here to help! Before returning seeds or if you didn't get a harvest:
- Take photos of affected plants
- Note what variety and when you sowed
- Email growing@fernhollow.example
- Visit us on Saturdays to discuss

Remember: every gardener has failures. What matters is what you learn.
