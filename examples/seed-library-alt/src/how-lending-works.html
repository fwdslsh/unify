How Lending Works

Fernhollow keeps its collection alive through a cycle of borrowing, growing, and sharing. Our system is designed to be simple and accessible for all gardeners.

The borrowing process

  1. Visit us: Come to our Saturday morning sessions during the growing season (10am–2pm)
  2. Choose your seeds: Browse our catalogue and select varieties that suit your garden
  3. Record your loan: We keep track of what you've borrowed so we know what to expect back
  4. Take them home: Follow the growing guides and care for your plants

Returning seeds

When your plants go to seed, save some of the best specimens. Bring them back to us in clean, labeled packets. You can return as little as a small handful—we're not expecting commercial quantities.

What makes it work

  • Everyone benefits from everyone else's growing successes
  • We adapt seeds to our local climate year by year
  • Seed keepers maintain detailed notes on each variety
  • No cash is needed—just commitment to the cycle

Membership terms

General principles

Fernhollow keeps membership simple and open. By borrowing seeds, you agree to:

  • Grow the seeds in good faith
  • Return seeds when they mature, or report what happened if you couldn't grow them
  • Keep seeds clean and properly labeled when returning
  • Share knowledge with other gardeners in our community

Seed quality standards

Returned seeds should be:

  • Fully mature and dried
  • Free from disease or pest damage
  • Labeled with variety name and date
  • Stored in paper packets (not plastic)

If you can't return seeds

Life happens. If you're unable to save seeds, just let us know. You can still return as a member next season, and we'll work with you to find varieties that suit your situation better.

Our commitment to you

We maintain accurate records, provide growing support, and celebrate the successes of our community. Your privacy is protected—member contact details are never shared outside our core group.
