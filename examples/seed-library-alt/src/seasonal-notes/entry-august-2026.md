title: "August update: Peak harvest season"
---

**August 10, 2026**

Fernhollow keeps summer at full intensity right now. The combination of long days, recent rain, and established root systems has everything producing at peak levels.

## What's happening in our gardens

**Tomatoes:** Our keepers report excellent flowering and fruit setting on all varieties. The Black Krim and Aunt Ruby are particularly vigorous—some plants are already shoulder-high. Stupice is producing clusters of small fruit that are just beginning to color.

**Beans:** Lazy Housewife and Cherokee Wax are in full production. Several keepers report harvesting daily. The flowers are beautiful this year—they're setting well despite the heat.

**Squash:** Uchiki Kuri plants are sprawling beautifully. Early female flowers are forming. We expect first harvest by late August.

**Brassicas:** The late-sown kales from June are now bushy transplants, ready for their autumn growth. R. Simms reports her Red Russian is looking particularly vigorous.

## Weather notes

The dry spell in July has given way to gentle rain. This is ideal for seed production—consistent moisture without waterlogging.

## Member spotlight

T. Okonkwo brought in photos of her Stupice tomatoes. The plants are growing in large containers and producing more fruit than she expected. She's planning to save seed from several fruits and will return a full packet in autumn.

## Looking ahead

By September, we'll shift focus to autumn crops. If you want to borrow varieties for fall and winter growing, now is the time to contact us. We're already planning distribution for the September seed update.

## Next update

We'll report on first harvests and seed maturity next month.
