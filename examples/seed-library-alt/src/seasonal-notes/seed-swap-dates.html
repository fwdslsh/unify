Seed Swap Dates

Fernhollow keeps our community connected through regular gathering times. Join us to borrow seeds, return your harvest, and meet other gardeners.

Regular Saturday sessions

Every Saturday during the growing season

  • Time: 10am–2pm
  • Location: Fernhollow Community Garden, Valley Road
  • What to bring: Completed seed packets from last season, or just come to browse

No booking needed—just drop in! We're there rain or shine.

Growing season schedule

  • Spring season starts: April 1
  • Peak season: May through August
  • Autumn plantings: September through October
  • Winter closure: November through March

Special events

  • Seed swaps: First Saturday of each month during season (11am–1pm)
  • Growing workshops: Hosted by our keepers, dates announced monthly
  • Harvest celebration: September, date TBA
