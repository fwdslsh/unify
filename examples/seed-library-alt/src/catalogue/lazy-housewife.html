About this variety

Lazy Housewife has been grown in the Fernhollow valley since the 1920s and keeps its vigour when saved year on year.

Getting started

Ready to grow Lazy Housewife? Visit us on a Saturday (10am–2pm) during the growing season to borrow a packet. Check our growing guides for detailed instructions on starting from seed and saving seeds.

After you harvest

When your plants mature, save seeds following our seed-saving guide. Return them in a clean, labeled packet during your next visit, and your contribution will help next season's gardeners.
