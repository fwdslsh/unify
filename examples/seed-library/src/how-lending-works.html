How lending works

Fernhollow keeps lending simple: join, borrow, grow, and — when your plot allows — give a little seed back.

Joining

Membership is free and open to anyone gardening in the valley. Sign up at a Saturday opening; there is no waiting list.

Borrowing

Members may borrow up to five packets per visit. Take what you can sow this season — packets left ungrown help no one.

Giving back

You are never required to return seed, but a library only stays a library if some of it comes back. If you save seed from a variety you borrowed, bring a labelled envelope to any Saturday opening.

Membership terms

Membership is free and carries no obligation to return seed. Borrowed varieties remain the property of the seed keeper who holds them until any returned seed is logged. Members agree not to sell seed borrowed from the library and to label anything they bring back with variety and season.

These terms replace the usual contact strip on this page only; every other page carries the library's regular contact details.
