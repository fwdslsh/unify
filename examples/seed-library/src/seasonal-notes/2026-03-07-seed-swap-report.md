Posted 7 March 2026.

Fernhollow keeps count at every swap out of curiosity as much as anything, and
this morning's tally was the busiest spring swap in a few years — the main room
was full by half nine. Thank you to everyone who brought labelled seed to share,
and to everyone who took the time to write clear labels on it.

The next swap is in June — see the seed swap dates page for the full list.
