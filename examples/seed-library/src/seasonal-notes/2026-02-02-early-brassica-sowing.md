---
title: Time to start early brassicas
date: 2026-02-02
---

Posted 2 February 2026.

Fernhollow keeps a few varieties with sowing windows that open earlier than most
people expect, and Purple Sprouting is one of them — its window opens this month
if you're sowing under cover. Check the sowing dates on each variety's own
catalogue page before you start; they vary more than the general "brassica
season" suggests.

If you don't have space under cover, the library has two spare cold frames going
begging — ask at the Saturday opening.
