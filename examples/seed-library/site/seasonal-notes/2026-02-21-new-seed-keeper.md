---
title: Welcoming a new seed keeper
date: 2026-02-21
---

Posted 21 February 2026.

Fernhollow keeps its varieties true to type because each one has a member looking
after it season to season, and this week the library welcomed another seed keeper
to that rota. It's a small commitment — mostly just remembering to save some seed
back and keeping half an eye on how a variety performs — and we're always glad of
another pair of hands.

If you've been growing the same variety for a few years running and would like to
take one on officially, have a word at any Saturday opening.
