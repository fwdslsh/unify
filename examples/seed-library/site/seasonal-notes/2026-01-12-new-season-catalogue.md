title: This season's catalogue is up
date: 2026-01-12
---

Posted 12 January 2026.

Fernhollow keeps its catalogue current from the first Saturday of the year, and
this season's list is now on the shelves and on the website. Twenty-seven
varieties this time, across six families, with a few new additions from members
who saved seed over the autumn.

Come and see us on a Saturday morning if you'd like a paper copy — the noticeboard
by the door always has the latest one pinned up too.
